Why Sponsor LogiPharma Europe?

Why should my company sponsor LogiPharma Europe?

Sponsoring LogiPharma Europe gives solution providers an opportunity to connect with professionals working across pharmaceutical supply chains, logistics, distribution, manufacturing and operations.

For companies looking to increase their visibility within the pharmaceutical and life sciences industry, sponsorship provides a platform to showcase products, demonstrate expertise and build relationships with potential customers and partners.

Whether your business specialises in cold chain logistics, supply chain software, temperature monitoring, packaging or consulting, LogiPharma brings together an audience focused on solving the challenges facing pharmaceutical supply chains.

What makes LogiPharma Europe different from other pharmaceutical supply chain conferences?

LogiPharma Europe focuses on the end-to-end pharmaceutical and life sciences supply chain, bringing together professionals responsible for planning, manufacturing, logistics, distribution, procurement and digital transformation.

Rather than focusing exclusively on pharmaceutical manufacturing or product development, LogiPharma explores how organisations can build more resilient, efficient, sustainable and technology-enabled supply chains.

This makes the event particularly relevant for solution providers whose products and services address operational and supply chain challenges within pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies.

Is LogiPharma Europe suitable for pharmaceutical logistics and cold chain solution providers?

Yes. LogiPharma Europe is relevant to companies providing pharmaceutical logistics, temperature-controlled transportation, cold chain packaging, real-time shipment monitoring, freight forwarding, warehousing and distribution solutions.

The conference covers supply chain challenges such as product integrity, logistics visibility, distribution efficiency, regulatory compliance and supply chain resilience.

For cold chain and logistics solution providers, sponsoring LogiPharma offers an opportunity to demonstrate how their technologies and services can support pharmaceutical supply chain performance.

Which types of companies should consider sponsoring LogiPharma Europe?

LogiPharma Europe sponsorship is relevant to a wide range of pharmaceutical and life sciences solution providers, including:

  • Third-party logistics (3PL) and freight forwarding companies
  • Cold chain transportation and packaging providers
  • Temperature monitoring and shipment visibility technology companies
  • Supply chain planning and optimisation software providers
  • Warehouse automation and robotics companies
  • Pharmaceutical packaging and distribution specialists
  • AI, data analytics and digital supply chain technology providers
  • Supply chain consulting and professional services firms

Companies looking to develop relationships with pharmaceutical manufacturers and demonstrate solutions to industry-specific challenges may benefit from participating as sponsors or exhibitors.

Who Attends LogiPharma Europe?

Who attends LogiPharma Europe?

LogiPharma Europe attracts professionals from pharmaceutical, biotechnology and life sciences organisations, including leaders responsible for supply chain management, logistics, manufacturing, distribution, procurement, quality and digital transformation.

For 2027, LogiPharma is expecting an audience of more than 2,500 leaders from over 50 countries, with 63% at Director level or above.

This international audience creates opportunities for sponsors to engage with professionals involved in shaping pharmaceutical supply chain strategies and operational decisions.

Which pharmaceutical companies attend LogiPharma Europe?

LogiPharma has featured senior speakers from major pharmaceutical and life sciences organisations, including Pfizer, Novartis, Bayer, GSK, Merck, Takeda and Bristol Myers Squibb.

Participation varies by year but is continuously growing. Teams of 6+ from companies are encouraged to join.

  • Supply Chain Directors / Heads Of
  • Heads of Procurement and Strategic Sourcing
  • Directors of Digital Supply Chain Transformation
  • Manufacturing and Operations Leaders
  • Quality and Regulatory Supply Chain Professionals

This makes the conference relevant to solution providers seeking conversations with senior industry professionals.

Does LogiPharma Europe attract pharmaceutical supply chain decision-makers?

Yes. LogiPharma Europe attracts senior professionals involved in pharmaceutical supply chain strategy, logistics, operations, procurement and technology transformation.

These roles can influence how pharmaceutical companies evaluate new technologies, select service providers and develop supply chain capabilities.

However, purchasing authority varies by organisation and individual role. Sponsors should review the audience profile with the LogiPharma team to understand the relevance of attendees to their particular products and services.

Is LogiPharma Europe relevant for companies targeting European pharmaceutical manufacturers?

Yes. LogiPharma Europe is particularly relevant to solution providers seeking to build relationships within the European pharmaceutical and life sciences supply chain market.

Hosted in Vienna, the event brings together an international audience of industry professionals to discuss operational challenges, emerging technologies and supply chain transformation.

Companies expanding into European pharmaceutical markets can use the conference to develop industry connections, increase brand awareness and understand the priorities of prospective customers.
